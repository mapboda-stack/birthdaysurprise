A letter for my joy

For Shalom, with love ♡

My joy, 🥹💜

Happy birthday, my love. I wish I could put into words everything I feel when I think about you, but sometimes the heart carries more than words can explain. Still, I want to try, because you deserve to be reminded of how special you are.

I am grateful that our paths crossed. I love getting to know you, seeing your face, hearing your voice, sharing little moments with you, and discovering more of the person you are. Even the ordinary moments mean something to me because they are moments I get to share with you.

On your birthday, I do not just want to celebrate the day you were born. I want to celebrate the life you are living and the person you are still becoming. There are beautiful things ahead of you, and I hope you never let a difficult season convince you that your story will not be beautiful.

Please remember that you do not have to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You can have difficult days, feel uncertain, change your mind, learn new things, and still be worthy of patience and kindness. When something hurts you, I want to keep learning how to listen instead of making you feel like your feelings are too much. I want my love to be something you can feel, not just something I say.

I pray that this new chapter brings you wisdom, opportunities, genuine happiness, good health, peace of mind, and the courage to pursue the dreams God has placed in your heart. May you find the right people, make beautiful memories, and grow into a woman who knows her worth without needing the world to prove it to her.

And as we keep growing, I hope we learn to love each other better: with honesty, understanding, patience, laughter, prayer, and actions that match our words. I do not expect us to know everything today. I just hope we keep choosing to learn and grow with sincerity.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the smiles, the conversations, the little things, and the chance to share a part of this journey with you. I hope this birthday makes you feel celebrated, cherished, and reminded of how much your life means.

Happy birthday once again, my sunshine. May this be the beginning of a chapter filled with beautiful surprises, answered prayers, and reasons to thank God. I am cheering for you, today and in all the days ahead.

Forever is the deal. ♡

A blessing over your life

My birthday prayer for you.

More than gifts or beautiful words, I pray that God's presence guides you through this new year of your life.

Heavenly Father, thank You for Shalom. Thank You for the gift of her life, for bringing her this far, and for every lesson, every opportunity, every smile, and every moment that has helped shape the person she is today.

Lord, as she enters a new year, please go before her. Give her wisdom when she has decisions to make, courage when she feels afraid, strength when she feels tired, and peace when life feels uncertain. Protect her coming and going, guide her friendships, and surround her with people who genuinely want to see her grow.

“The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee.”

Numbers 6:24–25 · KJV (excerpt)

Father, please bless the work of her hands. Open the right doors for her future, help her recognise good opportunities, and give her discernment to walk away from what is not good for her. Let her education, career, gifts, talents, and dreams grow according to Your will. When plans change, remind her that she is not forgotten.

“For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.”

Jeremiah 29:11 · KJV

Lord, teach her to trust You with all her heart, especially when she cannot see the whole picture. Give her a heart that loves what is good, a mind filled with wisdom, and faith that remains steady through every season.

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.”

Proverbs 3:5 · KJV (excerpt)

Bless her health, her peace of mind, her family, her plans, and the quiet dreams she has not yet told anyone. Help her find joy in the journey, not only when she reaches the destination. Let this year bring growth without losing her peace, success without losing her character, and love that reflects patience, truth, and kindness.

And Father, if it is Your will for our relationship to grow into a lasting future, teach us to build it with wisdom, honesty, faithfulness, patience, and respect. Help us become better people individually and treat each other with the love we pray for.

May Shalom always know that her life has purpose, that she can come to You with her worries, and that she does not have to carry everything alone. Let her new year be full of Your guidance, favour, protection, and grace.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

Amen. 🤍
