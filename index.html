A Letter for My Joy

Take your time reading this, my love. I wrote it especially for you.

My dear Shalom, my joy, my sunshine,

Happy birthday, my love. 💜

I wanted to make something that would help you feel celebrated today, something you could return to whenever you need a reminder that your life matters and that there are beautiful things still ahead of you.

Today, I celebrate more than another year of your life. I celebrate the person you are: your own mind, your hopes, your personality, and all the little things that make you uniquely you. I hope you never feel that you must become somebody else to deserve good things. You can keep growing without losing the person you are today.

My joy, please don't be too hard on yourself when life feels slow. You don't need to have every answer right now, and you don't have to compare your journey with somebody else's. People grow through different seasons, and not every kind of progress is visible to the world.

Keep taking your dreams seriously. Keep learning, keep preparing yourself, and keep believing that your effort matters. Celebrate the little wins, too. The days you show up when you feel tired, the things you learn after a mistake, and the courage it takes to begin again are all worth recognising.

I hope you also remember to take care of yourself along the way. Rest when you need to. Give yourself grace when things don't work out exactly as planned. You can be ambitious and still be gentle with yourself. You can be strong and still ask for help.

Thank you for being part of my life. I hope this gift reminds you that you deserve appreciation not only when you achieve something, but simply because you are you. I want you to keep discovering your strengths, becoming more confident, and making choices that reflect the future you want.

"You do not have to rush into your future to be worthy of a beautiful one."

So today, smile a little longer. Enjoy your birthday. Receive the love around you, and give yourself permission to be happy. Your story is still unfolding, Shalom, and I hope you walk into this new chapter with faith, hope, courage, and a heart that knows its worth.

Happy birthday, my sunshine. May this new year of your life bring you peace, good health, wisdom, meaningful friendships, spiritual growth, and many beautiful reasons to thank God.

With love, always,

Your boyfriend, Paul. ♡
